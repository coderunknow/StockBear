## 2. Prospective performance/resource budget (checklist 6.8)

Historical exp-0055 had a calibrated cost measurement but **no numeric cost
budget**. Do not retroactively describe its measurement as budgeted. Its JSON
and report remain unchanged. Check 6.8 stays open until a new measurement is
made under this explicitly prospective budget on the unchanged candidate.

For exp-0059 and subsequent feature screens unless a stricter experiment-specific
budget is frozen:

1. Same binary, same fixed diagnostic corpus, alternating A/B order. At least
   60 A/A blocks and 90 A/B blocks. Each block is 12 complete evaluation passes.
2. A/A is interpretable only if `p90 − p10 < 0.10` and its median ratio is in
   `[0.98, 1.02]`. At most two calibration attempts; if still noisy, no cost
   claim and no strength-game dispatch until an isolated calibrated instrument
   is available. This is a measurement-quality rule, not a strength stopping rule.
3. Median new-feature evaluation-time increment **≤ 5%** over its immediate
   ablation. Median total enabled evaluation-time increment **≤ 10%** over the
   champion configuration. These are local overhead limits, not Elo estimates.
4. Preserve per-block raw A/B nanoseconds, input/binary/configuration hashes,
   seed/corpus, command, and build flags. A failed cost limit blocks games for
   that implementation; optimize without changing semantics, prove equivalence,
   and remeasure under the same limit. Never relax the limit after observation.
5. No claim that evaluation overhead equals node-time overhead or strength.
   Actual fixed-time games remain the only strength evidence.

The exp-0059 corpus is 4,000 positions regenerated by
`check_passed_pawn_race.random_positions(4000, 20261116)` and committed as an
EPD with its SHA-256. This is the same deterministic corpus specification as
the historical diagnostic, not a selected favorable subset.

## 3. Numeric game-compute budget and stopping

Every new plan must declare its exact game count, job count, CI timeout ceiling,
and number of benchmark/export dispatches. No adaptive additional games, no optional
confirmation after a rejected screen, and no repeated sampling until favorable.
A null failure is **INVALID**, not permission to rerun the same experiment.
Infrastructure failure before any game may be retried once with the original
plan and the failure documented; partial-game reruns require a new identity
and a technical reason, never pooling or selecting the favorable attempt.

For exp-0059 specifically:

- Exactly **7,800 games**: 6,000 primary + 1,200 candidate null + 600 stage0.
- Exactly **four** isolated benchmark jobs, `timeout-minutes: 420` each;
  one benchmark dispatch and one evidence-export dispatch.
- Upper bound **1,680 benchmark VM-minutes**, plus the existing prepare/export
  job ceilings (10 minutes each): **1,700 workflow VM-minutes**. Actual public
  GitHub-hosted runners only; no paid service.
- One fixed frozen sample. Stop at completion (or technical failure), export
  once, audit every validity condition, apply the frozen decision, and advance
  to a different direction if not confirmed. No result-driven retry.
