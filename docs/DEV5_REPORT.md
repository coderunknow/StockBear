## Verdict questions

1. **Is dev5 stronger than dev4 by the gate (fixed-time 40+4 ms, opening-clustered
   two-sided 95% CI lower bound strictly > +50 Elo)?** No. Confirmation on the
   untouched 550-family suite (seed 2026110205, 1100 games, run alone, null first):
   candidate (pawn-safe mobility build `13d3a684...`) vs dev4 (`1c5b61a7...`)
   = +15.9 [+0.6, +31.2] clustered, pentanomial [+0.9, +30.9], WDL 413-321-364,
   2 unadjudicated 240-ply games, no forfeits
   (`benchmarks/results/0.1.0-dev5/confirm-safe-time40_4.*`). Same-binary null
   (safe vs safe) = +5.4 [−9.8, +20.6] (`confirm-null-time40_4.*`). A point
   estimate is not the gate; the lower bound +0.6 fails it by a wide margin.
2. **Best measured dev5-vs-dev4 numbers anywhere in the cycle?** Fixed-node
   diagnostics (not gate evidence): `EvalParams=params_outcome` +60.7
   [+36.1,+85.9] at n8000, +25.1 [+5.7,+44.6] at n3000/960 g, +29.9 [+5.7,+54.4]
   at n20000. Fixed-time clean screens at 40+4: outcome params +9.4
   [−13.9,+32.9]; safe mobility +2.2 [−21.0,+25.3]. Fixed time is the primary
   evidence class and it does not approach +50.
3. **What was built and tried?** (a) classical Texel-style retuning of the
   32-feature eval on 479,666 self-play positions with SF11 search/outcome
   labels (4 targets; only the outcome arm beat dev4); (b) NNUE nets trained
   from scratch on 309,998 SF11 labels (buckets 1/4/16, wdl/cp targets,
   weight decay, output-scale re-quantization sweep); (c) isolated search
   switches re-screened (MoveCountPruning −34.9 rejected; others ≤ +6);
   (d) pawn-safe mobility (compile-time candidate); (e) `FullSoftBudget`
   time-management switch (implemented, default off, rejected at 40+4:
   −21.0 [−44.4,+2.1] with outcome params vs +9.4 without).
4. **Why did NNUE fail?** Engine inference is bit-exact vs the independent
   python evaluator; the trained nets are simply a weaker evaluator in real
   play (on 910 game positions: corr with outcome 0.473 vs classical 0.646,
   Brier 0.205 vs 0.099, output std 1127 cp vs 470 cp) and lose −650..−760 at
   every depth (n3000/n8000/n50000) and output scale (285.71/150/75). Abandoned.
5. **Why did the fixed-node gains not transfer to fixed time?** At 40+4 ms the
   soft budget is only ~4 ms (≈7k nodes/move, depth 3-7); early soft stops, clock
   overhead and node-count variance dilute the fixed-node effect: the best
   eval candidate measures ≈ +9 clean at 40+4 vs +60.7 at fixed n8000.

## Bottom line

The dev5 cycle produced one genuine, reproducible improvement signal —
outcome-label Texel tuning of the classical eval (+25..+61 Elo at fixed node
counts) — but at the gate's fixed-time 40+4 ms condition every candidate
measured within noise of +2..+16 Elo, and the pre-registered confirmation
lower bound is +0.6. The >+50 hard gate is **not met**; the honest verdict is
FAILED/INCOMPLETE for this cycle, with the outcome-tuned eval as the most
promising direction for a future cycle (it needs the data pipeline re-run and
a fixed-time-first evaluation, since fixed-node gains did not transfer).
